## Tier 1 — High Automation, Fast Close

### 1. Voice AI Lead Intake
- **What:** AI phone answering that qualifies, routes, and books leads 24/7.
- **Why it sells:** Missed calls = lost revenue. Obvious, measurable ROI.
- **Recurring:** Monthly subscription.
- **4-Layer Fit:** ✅ LLM (conversation), ✅ RAG (business rules/scripts), ✅ Agent (call handling), ✅ Agentic (optimization over time).

### 2. Reputation Management
- **What:** Automated review generation, response, and recovery workflows.
- **Why it sells:** Every local business cares about Google reviews.
- **Recurring:** Monthly subscription.
- **4-Layer Fit:** ✅ All layers.

### 3. Lead Capture + Follow-Up Automation
- **What:** Forms, chatbots, and automated follow-up sequences that convert visitors to booked calls.
- **Why it sells:** Most businesses leak leads because follow-up is manual and slow.
- **Recurring:** Monthly subscription.

### 4. Appointment Booking Automation
- **What:** AI-powered scheduling that eliminates phone tag.
- **Why it sells:** Time savings are immediately felt.
- **Recurring:** Monthly subscription.

## Offer Selection Criteria
Every offer CloserForge sells must pass:
1. **LLM Fit** — Can AI reasoning/generation power the core value?
2. **RAG Fit** — Can delivery be grounded in reusable knowledge?
3. **Agent Fit** — Can the workflow be executed by tools and APIs?
4. **Agentic Fit** — Can the system manage lifecycle with minimal human touch?
5. **Recurring Revenue** — Does it convert to MRR?
6. **Niche Language** — Has niche research been completed first?

If an offer fails any of these, it doesn't ship.
