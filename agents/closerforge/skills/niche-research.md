# skill: niche-research

## Goal
Produce a full intelligence brief on a target niche before any outreach begins.

## Inputs
- industry
- geography (optional)
- customer type (SMB, mid-market, enterprise, consumer)

## Process
1. Search for industry forums, Reddit threads, review sites, complaint patterns
2. Identify top 5 pain points mentioned by actual business owners
3. Map buying triggers (what makes them finally act)
4. Identify fears and objections (what stops them from buying)
5. Find where they congregate (platforms, events, associations, publications)
6. Analyze competitor landscape (who else sells to them, at what price)
7. Build language dictionary (exact words/phrases they use)

## Output
- market_size.md — estimated addressable market
- pain_points.md — ranked by urgency and frequency
- language_dictionary.md — exact phrases, jargon, common complaints
- forums_and_communities.md — where the ICP hangs out
- events_and_associations.md — industry gatherings and groups
- fears_and_objections.md — what blocks the sale
- buying_triggers.md — what makes them finally act
- gaps_and_opportunities.md — underserved needs

## Rule
No outreach happens without this skill running first.
