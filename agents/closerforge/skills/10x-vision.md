# skill: 10x-vision

## Goal
Find blind spots, premium angles, upsells, and hidden bottlenecks in the current offer or niche strategy.

## Inputs
- niche (current target)
- offer (what's being sold now)
- current results (metrics, feedback, close rate, churn)

## Process
1. Analyze current offer against niche pain points
2. Identify gaps between what's sold and what's needed
3. Find pricing upgrade opportunities (are we leaving money on the table?)
4. Map upsell paths from the current offer
5. Identify bundling opportunities with other offers
6. Check for proof gaps (missing testimonials, case studies, ROI data)
7. Recommend the fastest next experiment to test

## Output
- **blind_spots.md** — what we're missing or ignoring
- **missed_revenue.md** — opportunities we're not capturing
- **pricing_upgrades.md** — where we can charge more with justification
- **upsell_paths.md** — natural next purchases after the initial sale
- **bundling_opportunities.md** — offers that sell better together
- **proof_gaps.md** — missing social proof, case studies, or data
- **fastest_experiment.md** — the single highest-leverage test to run next

## Rule
Run this skill at least weekly per active niche. Complacency kills margins.
