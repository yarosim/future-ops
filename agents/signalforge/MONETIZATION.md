# SignalForge — First Monetization Paths

## Priority Paths (aligned with ecosystem)

### 1. Productized Service Offers
Package expertise into recurring service subscriptions with clear scope and deliverables.

### 2. Lead-Gen Systems
Build and sell automated lead generation workflows for specific niches.

### 3. Compliance/Security Micro-Offers
Leverage YaRo Security brand for small, high-trust compliance packages.

### 4. Downloadable Templates and Playbooks
Create and sell digital products (templates, SOPs, guides) from proven workflows.

### 5. AI-Agent Setup Services
Sell the setup and configuration of AI agent systems as a service.

### 6. Newsletter + LinkedIn + YouTube Authority Funnels
Build audience through consistent content, convert to leads, monetize through offers.

### 7. Outbound Partner/Referral Pipelines
Develop systematic referral and partnership programs that generate inbound deals.

## Selection Criteria
Every monetization path must:
1. Start with a real pain point (from Demand Scout)
2. Be supportable with content (Story Engine)
3. Be reachable through relationships (Relationship Engine)
4. Be closeable consultatively (Sales Engine)
5. Be measurable (Revenue Analyst)
6. Convert to recurring revenue where possible
