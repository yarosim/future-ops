# Engine 5: Revenue Analyst

## Purpose
Scores what produced clicks, leads, calls, and cash. Feeds insights back into the next cycle.

## Process
1. Pull performance data: impressions, clicks, leads, calls, conversions, revenue
2. Rank channels by ROI
3. Rank offers by conversion rate and margin
4. Identify best-performing stories and hooks
5. Identify stalled leads for reactivation
6. Recommend what to stop, scale, or replace
7. Feed findings back into Demand Scout and Story Engine

## Outputs
- revenue_memo.md (weekly)
- channel_rankings.md
- offer_performance.md
- stalled_leads.md
- recommendations.md

## Frequency
Daily metrics check, weekly deep analysis, monthly strategy reset.
