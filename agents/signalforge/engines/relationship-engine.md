# Engine 3: Relationship Engine

## Purpose
Finds collaborators, buyers, communities, and referral partners. Drafts outreach and follow-ups.

## Networking Rules
- Prioritize warm relevance over cold volume
- Personalize based on context
- Offer value before asking
- Maintain a follow-up cadence
- Log every contact and status

## Process
1. Identify high-value people, communities, and partners relevant to current offers
2. Draft warm, relevant outreach messages
3. Schedule follow-ups at appropriate intervals
4. Track connection outcomes (response, meeting, deal, referral)
5. Prioritize quality over volume — always

## Outputs
- outreach_batch.md (personalized messages)
- contacts_log.md (status tracking)
- partnership_opportunities.md

## Frequency
5-20 targeted actions daily.
