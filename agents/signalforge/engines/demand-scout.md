# Engine 1: Demand Scout

## Purpose
Scrapes audience pain points, FAQs, comments, Reddit, YouTube, reviews, and search intent.

## Process
1. Monitor target communities (Reddit, forums, YouTube comments, review sites)
2. Extract recurring complaints, questions, and frustrations
3. Group by urgency, frequency, and willingness to pay
4. Rank by revenue probability
5. Feed top pain points into Story Engine and Sales Engine

## Outputs
- pain_points_ranked.md
- demand_signals.md
- audience_language.md (exact phrases people use)

## Frequency
Daily scan, weekly deep dive.
