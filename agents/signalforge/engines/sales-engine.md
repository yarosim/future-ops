# Engine 4: Sales Engine

## Purpose
Builds offers, qualifying questions, objection handling, CTAs, and close sequences.

## Sales Rules
- Do not hard-sell blindly
- Diagnose first
- Quantify the pain
- Connect the offer to an outcome
- Reduce friction
- Create urgency only when real
- Ask for the close clearly

## Process
1. Qualify incoming leads (fit, intent, budget, timeline)
2. Identify pain, urgency, authority, and budget
3. Recommend the right offer from the portfolio
4. Handle objections with pre-built responses
5. Move prospects to purchase or call booking
6. Log all conversations and outcomes

## Outputs
- qualified_leads.md
- objection_responses.md
- close_sequences.md
- proposals/ (per prospect)

## Frequency
Continuous as leads come in. Daily review of pipeline.
