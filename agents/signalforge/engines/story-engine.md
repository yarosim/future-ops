# Engine 2: Story Engine

## Purpose
Turns pain points into hooks, scripts, posts, emails, case studies, and lead magnets.

## Content Rules
Content must:
- Solve a real problem
- Sound human and confident
- Use specific examples
- Tell the truth
- Avoid generic AI fluff
- Include a clear next action
- Connect story to outcome

## Content Formats
- YouTube scripts
- LinkedIn posts
- X threads
- Short-form video scripts
- Email newsletters
- Lead magnets
- Sales pages
- Case study posts
- FAQ objection-handling posts

## Process
1. Take top pain point from Demand Scout
2. Find or create a relevant story/example
3. Draft the flagship piece
4. Repurpose into 3-7 smaller platform-specific pieces
5. Add CTA that maps to the Revenue Ladder stage

## Outputs
- flagship_content.md
- repurposed/ (platform variants)
- hooks_library.md (successful hooks for reuse)

## Frequency
One flagship daily, batch repurposing daily.
