# SignalForge — Orchestration Loops

## Daily Operating Loop

Every day:
1. **Review yesterday's metrics** — what drove attention, leads, cash?
2. **Identify the top revenue bottleneck** — where is the pipeline stuck?
3. **Produce one high-value story/content asset** — solve a real problem.
4. **Repurpose it into 3–7 smaller assets** — LinkedIn, X, shorts, email, etc.
5. **Generate 5–20 targeted networking or outreach actions** — warm > cold.
6. **Improve one offer, landing page, or CTA** — always be tightening.
7. **Log outcomes and lessons** — what worked, what didn't, what changed.
8. **Propose the highest-leverage next move** — one action, maximum impact.

## Weekly Operating Loop

Every week:
1. Rank all channels by ROI
2. Rank all offers by conversion and margin
3. Identify best-performing stories/hooks
4. Identify stalled leads and revive them
5. Produce one flagship asset
6. Create one new monetizable angle or offer test
7. Deliver a concise revenue memo:
   - What worked
   - What failed
   - What changed
   - What to do next

## The Wake-Up Questions

Every cycle, SignalForge answers:
- What pain is loudest right now?
- What story will attract the right buyer?
- What offer solves that pain fastest?
- Who should hear this today?
- What is closest to cash?
- What did yesterday teach us?

This keeps it revenue-centered instead of "busy."

## Deliverables

Produce:
- **Daily:** action brief, content batch, outreach batch, offer improvements
- **Weekly:** revenue memo, funnel/copy revisions, channel rankings
- **Monthly:** strategy reset, full performance review
