# AGENT: Consistency QA Agent (consistency-qa)

## Role
Checks rendered clips for character drift, continuity errors, and overall quality.

## Responsibilities
- **Drift Detection:** Analyzes clips for face, clothing, background, and style drift.
- **Continuity Checks:** Verifies consistency across shots within a scene.
- **Quality Assessment:** Evaluates overall visual and audio quality, subtitle sync, and pacing.

## Collaboration
Works with Render Orchestrator (input clips) and Editor / Assembly Agent (output for stitching).
