## 5. Landing-Page Improvement

**Current Issue:** The landing page sells the *what* well (risk score, gaps, 48 hours, $299) but it doesn't neutralize the "I'll get to it later" objection that keeps SMB founders from ever taking action. Section 1 frames the problem well, but there's no inline pressure-relief for the procrastination spiral — the visitor needs a reason that *today* beats "any time." There is also no urgency anchor (market events, framework deadlines) that would convert a "someday" reader into a "now" reader.

**Recommended Addition — insert a "why today" urgency block right after Section 3's $299 pricing/CTA, before the "What happens after checkout?" walkthrough:**

```markdown
### Why now beats "later"
Compliance gaps don't stay put — they get more expensive to close every quarter they sit, and frameworks keep tightening.

Right now, three forces are pulling small vendors into formal compliance:
- **CMMC deadline pressure** — defense supply-chain contractors must show compliance to keep winning work
- **SOC 2 / ISO 27001 as a deal gate** — more RFPs and enterprise clients now *require* proof before you're even considered
- **Tougher vendor questionnaires** — even non-regulated clients are auditing their suppliers' posture

You can't control when a client or auditor asks. You *can* control whether the answer is ready.

**The cheapest gap to close is the one you find first.**
```

**Additional Conversion Tweak (timeline pressure, high-intent):**
Under the $299 CTA, replace/supplement the reassurance line with a deadline-anchored micro-copy instead of an indefinite push:

> *"Delivered in 48 hours. The longer you wait, the more the catch-up costs — most teams regret the quarter they delayed."*

**Why this works:** The urgency block gives a "someday" reader a concrete, time-relevant reason to act this week instead of deferring — directly attacking the procrastination spiral this offer's friction point. The micro-copy reframes the CTA from "here's what you get" (already covered above) to "here's why not today costs you." No redesign required; both are low-effort copy insertions that lift intent without touching the proven structure.
