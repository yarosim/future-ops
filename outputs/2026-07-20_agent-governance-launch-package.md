## Recommended launch sequence

1. Internally run the scorecard against one YaRo workflow and produce a redacted sample executive memo.
2. Confirm contracting, secure evidence-transfer, insurance, and data-retention terms before accepting sensitive engagements.
3. Manually reverify each priority account’s source and current offering immediately before personalized outreach.
4. Begin with MSP design-partner conversations; the channel path can create multiple downstream client audits.
5. In parallel, approach regulated platforms that publicly mention AI/agents and a small set of autonomy-focused defense firms.
6. Do not claim compliance, certification, breach prevention, or knowledge of a prospect’s internal control gaps.

## Claims and compliance notes

- The scorecard is triage, not certification, legal advice, penetration testing, or proof of security.
- Control mappings in the sprint are reference mappings only.
- Prospect rationale is based on public materials and is not evidence that a company uses a particular internal agent stack or has a vulnerability.
- Outreach should be organization-level, relevant, respectful, and compliant with applicable law and platform rules. This package intentionally contains no personal contact data.
