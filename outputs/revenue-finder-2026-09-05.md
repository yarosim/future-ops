## 5 concrete sellable opportunities

### 1. AI Agent Governance & "Bounded Autonomy" Audit — the anti-cancellation layer
- **Why now:** Gartner's 40%-cancellation prediction + 51% of MSPs naming governance as their top AI barrier. Everyone is deploying agents; almost nobody has guardrails, audit trails, prompt versioning, or rollback.
- **What to sell:** A fixed-fee **Agent Governance & Compliance Readiness Assessment** — audit existing/planned agent deployments, map permissions, build policy templates (pre-approved / human-review / prohibited actions), stand up logging + escalation paths.
- **Buyer:** CIOs/CISOs at mid-market; MSPs rolling out agentic service desks.
- **Revenue model:** $5–15k assessment → $2–5k/mo ongoing governance retainer.
- **Fit for YaRo Security:** Highest. This is native security/compliance territory and positions you as the "safe way to do AI agents."

### 2. MSP Compliance-as-a-Service (CaaS) Enablement Kit
- **Why now:** Only 36% of MSPs offer formal compliance; 21% growth projected; 94% want to automate but 43% can deliver. Boutique MSPs can't build this alone.
- **What to sell:** White-label compliance automation stack for MSPs — automated evidence collection, continuous monitoring, audit-readiness reporting, policy management. "ISO 27001 ready in 8 weeks" outcome framing.
- **Buyer:** MSPs with regulated clients (healthcare/HIPAA, fintech/PCI, manufacturing/ITAR-NIST).
- **Revenue model:** Platform/partner fee + per-client CaaS line item.
- **Fit:** Strong — pairs with #1 (governance) and leverages YaRo's security credibility.

### 3. GovCon CMMC / NIST 800-171 Compliance-Agent (security-specific)
- **Why now:** Generic GovCon agents (GovDash, CLEATUS, Hamilton) cover BD/capture/proposal but under-serve the **security compliance** angle. CMMC 2.0 + NIST 800-171 evidence collection is a painful, recurring, high-value gap.
- **What to sell:** An agent that auto-collects CMMC/NIST 800-171 evidence, builds the compliance matrix, watches for control drift, and produces audit-ready packages — the security vertical of the GovCon agent boom.
- **Buyer:** Small/mid federal contractors (SAM.gov registered, ~hundreds of thousands).
- **Revenue model:** $99–499/mo per seat, or project + retainer.
- **Fit:** Excellent — direct security specialization, defensible against generic GovCon tools.

### 4. CRM/RevOps Hygiene + Pipeline Agent (bounded autonomy)
- **Why now:** CRM-hygiene agent is the agreed first RevOps deployment; 40% of enterprise apps embedding agents by end of 2026; Agentforce at $1.4B ARR validates demand.
- **What to sell:** A governed RevOps agent doing pipeline hygiene, lead routing/scoring, forecast refresh — with audit trails and human checkpoints (the "bounded autonomy" differentiator), not a black box.
- **Buyer:** RevOps leads / CROs at B2B SaaS.
- **Revenue model:** $1–3k/mo per deployment.
- **Fit:** Moderate — more adjacent to YaRo's core, but the governance angle keeps it on-brand.

### 5. Document & Evidence Extraction Automation (high-volume compliance parsing)
- **Why now:** Cohere Parse 5 launched this week targeting messy enterprise documents; tender/invoice/contract processing runs ~90% faster with ~95% extraction accuracy. Compliance evidence collection is a natural high-volume target.
- **What to sell:** Automated evidence collection + document parsing for audits (SOC 2, ISO 27001, CMMC) — turn the "screenshot and spreadsheet" grind into continuous, code-driven compliance.
- **Buyer:** Compliance teams, MSPs, GovCon contractors.
- **Revenue model:** Per-document or per-client retainer.
- **Fit:** Strong — directly supports #2 and #3; a component not a standalone.

## Recommended offer/campaign for YaRo Security

**"Agent Governance & Compliance-as-a-Service for MSPs & GovCon"**

Lead with **Opportunity #1 (Agent Governance Audit)** as the wedge, bundled with the CaaS enablement (#2) and CMMC/NIST evidence automation (#3/#5). This is the single most defensible position: YaRo Security is a security firm, and the #1 unmet need across both MSPs and GovCon contractors in 2026 is **safe, governed, audit-ready AI agent deployment**.

**Campaign structure:**
1. **Offer:** Fixed-fee **"Agent Governance & Compliance Readiness Assessment"** ($5–15k) — audit agent deployments, governance gaps, and compliance posture (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / CMMC / NIST 800-171).
2. **Conversion:** Assessment → monthly **Governance & Compliance retainer** ($2–5k/mo) covering guardrails, evidence automation, continuous monitoring, audit readiness.
3. **Target segments (in order):**
   - MSPs with regulated clients (HIPAA/PCI/ITAR) — sell CaaS enablement, "ISO 27001 ready in 8 weeks."
   - Small/mid federal contractors facing CMMC 2.0 + NIST 800-171 deadlines — sell CMMC evidence agent.
4. **Messaging hook:** *"40% of AI agent projects fail on governance. Make yours the exception — and get audit-ready while you're at it."*
5. **Proof point to lead with:** Only 36% of MSPs offer compliance services; 51% cite governance as their #1 AI barrier; 94% want to automate but only 43% can. The gap is the market.

**Why this wins:** It rides the strongest macro signal (agentic governance = the anti-cancellation wedge), it's native to YaRo's security/compliance identity, it converts a one-time assessment into recurring revenue, and it's differentiated against both generic GovCon agents and generic compliance SaaS by combining **security + governance + automation** in one offer.
