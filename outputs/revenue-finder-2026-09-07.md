## 5 Concrete Sellable Opportunities

### 1. 🛡️ AI Agent Governance & Compliance-as-a-Service ("SOC 2 for AI agents")
- **Why now:** AICPA draft guidance (Mar 2026) makes AI agent audit trails a SOC2 Type II requirement by **Q4 2026**. Deployments +340% vs governance +18%. Reddit/MarketCap both flag this becoming a procurement gate in ~18 months.
- **Sellable offer:** Subscription covering runtime audit trails (hash-chained, tamper-evident), policy enforcement, interrupt/override controls, and one-click SOC2/GDPR report generation from *real* agent activity (not consultant interviews).
- **Market:** $2.3B by 2028, 89% CAGR. SAM $180M (regulated industries).
- **Margins:** 25–45% gross (managed AI services tier).

### 2. 🏛️ NIST AI RMF 1.0 Readiness for GovCon / Federal Contractors
- **Why now:** NIST AI RMF 1.0 effective **Jan 2026** requires AI governance + audit trail for all production AI in federal contractors. Deltek: AI is GovCons' #1 priority, but only 25% have governance models. AI federal obligations $7.2B (1,743 contracts) with $91.8B potential award value.
- **Sellable offer:** Assessment + remediation + continuous compliance monitoring packaged as a recurring GovCon readiness line. "Win and keep federal work" — automates without losing control.
- **Why it wins:** Directly tied to revenue eligibility; buyers are motivated by lost-bid risk.

### 3. 🤖 Managed AI Runtime Governance Layer for MSPs (MIP play)
- **Why now:** 87% of MSPs expanding AI investment; MSPs pivoting to **Managed Intelligence Provider** — owning/billing the AI layer. Reddit's r/AI_Agents explicitly names runtime governance as "the missing layer."
- **Sellable offer:** White-label middleware that sits between MSP clients' agents and their actions — real-time policy check, pre-execution veto, drift monitoring, audit logs. Bundled into MSP subscriptions.
- **Margins:** 25–45% recurring; positions YaRo as infrastructure, not a one-off project.

### 4. 📞 AI Voice Agents + Compliance Bundling for MSPs
- **Why now:** Viirtue calls reselling AI Voice Agents the standout new recurring line — market $2.54B (2025) → $35.2B (2033), **39% CAGR**, and most MSPs haven't packaged it yet.
- **Sellable offer:** Wrap AI voice agents (phone-line revenue recovery, 24/7 coverage) with compliance recording/retention/consent so regulated clients (healthcare/finance) can deploy safely. Regulatory-ready = pricing premium + faster close.
- **Why it wins:** High margin, bills monthly, expands with call volume. Compliance wrapper is the differentiator generic resellers lack.

### 5. 🔍 Autonomous GovCon Opportunity Intelligence & Capture Agents
- **Why now:** Product Hunt/HN show demand for autonomous agents; GovCon firms flag opportunity ID (SAM.gov, forecasts, vehicle portals) as top manual pain. Only 5% of GovCons have mature AI — whitespace is wide.
- **Sellable offer:** Self-running agent that watches procurement sources, qualifies pursuits, builds compliance matrices from solicitations, drafts proposals from approved content — feeding YaRo's own autonomous-revenue thesis.
- **Why it wins:** Delivers a direct, measurable pipeline ROI and feeds opportunity #2 (governance compliance) as a cross-sell whenever an agent is sold.
