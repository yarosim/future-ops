## 5 Concrete Sellable Opportunities

### Opportunity 1: AI Agent SOC 2 & NIST RMF Audit Trail Wrapper for MSPs
* **Target Buyer:** Mid-market MSPs managing enterprise or regulated SMB clients deploying AI agents.
* **The Pain Point:** AICPA draft guidance (March 2026) dictates that AI agent audit trails become a SOC 2 Type II requirement by Q4 2026. MSPs are terrified of inheriting compliance liability for unmonitored agent actions.
* **Solution / Product:** A zero-friction middleware proxy + logging daemon that intercepts, logs, and enforces policy checkpoints (permission boundaries, human-in-the-loop override) on all AI agent tool calls, automatically generating auditor-ready compliance packets.
* **Monetization / Pricing:** $2,500 setup fee + $500/month per active agent instance under management.
* **Why Now:** Q4 2026 compliance deadlines are 60 days away; MSPs need immediate productized packaging to resell to clients.

### Opportunity 2: GovCon AI Proposal & Pricing Audit Defense Suite
* **Target Buyer:** Small-to-Mid Sized Government Contractors (GovCons).
* **The Pain Point:** 90% of GovCons use AI for proposal generation and pricing estimation (Deltek Clarity 2026), but fewer than 5% have mature governance. Federal auditors are aggressively demanding traceability on AI-assisted pricing and proposal compliance ("Show me how you priced this").
* **Solution / Product:** An automated compliance scrubber and provenance ledger that checks every AI-generated proposal section against FAR/DFARS clauses, tracking prompt history, source document validity, and human approval signatures into an immutable audit file.
* **Monetization / Pricing:** $15,000 annual SaaS license + per-proposal audit certification fee ($1,500/proposal).
* **Why Now:** 2026 Deltek Clarity report flags cybersecurity and pricing defensibility as the #1 audit risk area for federal contractors.

### Opportunity 3: Shadow AI & Non-Human Identity Discovery for MSPs
* **Target Buyer:** MSP Operations Directors and vCISOs.
* **The Pain Point:** Non-human identities (AI agents, API keys, MCP connectors) now outnumber human users in many SMBs, creating massive unmonitored attack surfaces and data leakage vectors.
* **Solution / Product:** A rapid automated scanner deployed via RMM/PSA that inventories every active AI tool, API key, and autonomous agent service account, risk-classifying their data access permissions and flagging over-privileged shadow AI.
* **Monetization / Pricing:** $3,500 one-time discovery audit per tenant, recurring as a monthly $750 continuous posture monitoring add-on.
* **Why Now:** Managed Services Journal highlights AI inventory and identity validation as the highest-margin frontier for 2026 MSP retainers.

### Opportunity 4: Autonomous CRM Deal Hygiene & Compliance Gatekeeper
* **Target Buyer:** Enterprise Sales Operations and Revenue Leaders.
* **The Pain Point:** Autonomous revenue ops agents updating CRMs (HubSpot/Salesforce) frequently push inaccurate data, bypass internal approval policies, or violate data privacy rules (GDPR/CCPA), risking broken pipeline forecasts.
* **Solution / Product:** A real-time policy enforcer sitting between sales AI agents and CRM APIs that validates deal stage movements, discount thresholds, and PII handling against corporate compliance rules before write operations occur.
* **Monetization / Pricing:** $1,200/month flat fee per 50 sales seats + tiered API call volume.
* **Why Now:** Gartner predicts 40% of organizations will scale back or remove AI agents due to failed operational governance and data pollution.

### Opportunity 5: Automated CMMC Level 1-2 AI Documentation Generator
* **Target Buyer:** GovCons and Defense Industrial Base (DIB) suppliers.
* **The Pain Point:** CMMC applicability continues to climb (reaching ~59% of contractors), yet maintaining continuous documentation of system security plans (SSPs) and POAMs eats massive internal engineering hours.
* **Solution / Product:** A secure, localized AI assistant pre-trained on NIST SP 800-171/CMMC requirements that continuously crawls internal repositories, ticket systems, and cloud configs to update artifact evidence automatically.
* **Monetization / Pricing:** $8,000 baseline implementation + $1,000/month compliance continuity retainer.
* **Why Now:** Heightened federal cybersecurity enforcement makes manual documentation an unsustainable operational bottleneck.
