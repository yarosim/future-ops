## Important interpretation

The **61/100 evidence-readiness score** is only a measure of how much relevant control evidence could be found and corroborated publicly. It is **not a compliance score, security rating, audit opinion, or statement about internal control effectiveness**.

Status language is intentionally constrained:

- **Evidence Found:** relevant public evidence was directly observed.
- **Not Publicly Verifiable:** evidence may exist internally, but was not established publicly.
- **Potential Gap:** public disclosure/evidence coverage appears incomplete; this is not proof of an operational deficiency.
- **Recommendation:** an evidence-readiness improvement, not a claim that Yahoo lacks the underlying control.
