## 5 concrete sellable opportunities

### 1) CMMC Phase 2 “CUI + AI Tool Scope” Readiness Sprint

**Buyer:** Small GovCon subcontractors, SDVOSBs, IT services firms, engineering/manufacturing suppliers handling CUI.  
**Trigger:** CMMC Phase 2 begins 2026-11-10; new solicitations increasingly require Level 2 C3PAO certification where CUI is involved. Reddit threads show contractors are still asking basic scope and clause questions.  
**Pain:** They do not know whether they are Level 1 or Level 2, where CUI lives, whether Copilot/ChatGPT/CRM tools touch CUI, or what needs to be isolated before an assessment.  
**Sellable package:**

- Contract/clause triage: FAR/DFARS/CMMC level interpretation.
- CUI data-flow map: email, SharePoint/OneDrive, CRM, tickets, file stores, vendors.
- AI/Copilot/agent exposure review: which tools can read/write sensitive data.
- Gap list mapped to NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2/CMMC Level 2 readiness.
- 30/60/90-day remediation roadmap and executive one-pager.

**Suggested price:** $1,500–$3,500 fixed fee; upsell to $997–$2,500/mo managed compliance support.  
**Why now:** Time-bound regulatory pressure plus confusion equals urgency.

- Client questionnaire for AI apps, automations, Copilot, browser agents, helpdesk bots.
- Microsoft 365/Purview baseline checklist.
- Least-privilege and approval-gate recommendations.
- Branded PDF report + remediation roadmap.
- Optional quarterly review cadence.

**Suggested price:** $2,500 setup for MSP + $500/client assessment, or revenue share per client.  
**Why now:** MSPs need something practical to sell before clients ask them to “secure AI.”

### 3) Microsoft 365 Copilot “Audit Before Pilot” Pack

**Sellable package:**

- SharePoint/OneDrive oversharing review.
- Purview Audit/eDiscovery availability check.
- Sensitivity label and DLP readiness review.
- CopilotInteraction audit validation.
- Pilot-risk report: “what Copilot can see before users go live.”

**Suggested price:** $1,250–$2,500 per tenant; recurring quarterly governance review.  
**Why now:** This is a low-friction, immediate security/compliance service for any Microsoft-heavy SMB.

### 4) CRM / Revenue Agent Safe-Autonomy Review

**Buyer:** RevOps leaders, founders, sales ops teams using Salesforce Agentforce, HubSpot AI, Slack GTM agents, outbound agents, or enrichment automations.  
**Trigger:** Product Hunt launches show CRM/GTM agents automating lead enrichment, follow-up, CRM hygiene, call context, and buying-signal routing. Salesforce is explicitly positioning Agentforce around trust, access controls, audit/feedback, Privacy Center, and compliance automation.  
**Pain:** Revenue teams want agent speed but fear bad sends, bad CRM writes, PII exposure, prospecting compliance issues, or untraceable autonomous actions.  
**Sellable package:**

- Agent workflow inventory: read-only, reversible, external-facing, irreversible.
- Approval thresholds for outbound/email/CRM-write actions.
- Lead-routing and PII access control review.
- Audit trail/evidence mapping for Salesforce/HubSpot/Slack workflows.
- “Safe autonomy” operating policy for RevOps agents.

**Suggested price:** $2,000–$5,000 depending on CRM complexity.  
**Why now:** RevOps agents are proliferating faster than governance habits.

### 5) Agent Identity + Kill Switch Mini-Audit

**Buyer:** Startups and SMBs with internal agents, n8n/Zapier workflows, service accounts, MCP servers, coding agents, or browser agents touching production systems.  
**Trigger:** Identity-security trend coverage is converging on agents as first-class identities. Enterprise buyers are starting to demand agent identity, named owners, least privilege, revocation paths, logs, and kill switches.  
**Pain:** Agents often run through shared human accounts or broad service accounts; no one can quickly revoke access or prove what the agent did.  
**Sellable package:**

- Inventory of agents, bots, service accounts, API tokens, MCP connections.
- Dedicated owner + purpose + data/action scope per agent.
- Shared-account and overprivilege findings.
- Kill-switch/revocation test for critical agents.
- Evidence pack for customer/procurement/security review.

**Suggested price:** $1,500–$4,000; strong upsell into managed monitoring/review.  
**Why now:** This is concrete, technical, and procurement-relevant — easier to sell than abstract “AI governance.”
