## Operating rule

No task is “progress” unless it changes a tracked state: **identified → qualified → prepared → approved → launched → engaged → converted**. Every claim must link to dated evidence.

## Daily control-loop template

1. **Verify:** What changed since the last timestamp?
2. **Diagnose:** What is the single binding constraint?
3. **Decide:** What work should stop, continue, or start?
4. **Assign:** Who owns the next action, and by when?
5. **Execute:** Advance one funnel stage.
6. **Measure:** Record the state change and evidence.
7. **Learn:** Log one reusable rule only when supported by evidence.

## KPIs for the next review

- Fresh scorecard completed: **target 1**
- Critical blockers with owners: **target 100%**
- Offers advanced at least one funnel stage: **target ≥1**
- Duplicate asset tasks initiated: **target 0**
- Unsupported operational claims: **target 0**
- Repeated blocker notifications without new information: **target 0**

## Failure triggers and correction

- **If the scorecard cannot be completed:** label missing data and create one collection task per missing critical metric.
- **If publishing remains blocked:** pivot to an approved channel or prepare the exact unblock step; do not generate more generic copy.
- **If no offer advances:** record why, identify the controlling dependency, and stop unrelated work until ownership is assigned.
- **If a cron output is stale:** do not reuse it as current evidence.
