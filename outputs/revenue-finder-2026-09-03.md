**Sellable deliverable:** 12-week "Agent Governance Readiness" engagement per the 5-layer framework:
- Agent identity & permission mapping (workload identity, least privilege, just-in-time creds)
- Action authorization controls + human approval checkpoints (which actions auto vs. human-gated)
