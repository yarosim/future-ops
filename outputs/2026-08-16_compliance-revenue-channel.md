## 5. Landing-page improvement

### Add a top-of-page risk anchor: "What it costs to wait" + a buyer question inset

**Where:** Above SECTION 1 (The Problem), before any scroll loss.

**Recommended inset (small, 2–3 lines):**

> **What it costs to wait:** Audits, customer security reviews, and RFPs don't reschedule because you weren't ready. A gap discovered late usually costs more than the $299 to find it early.

**Recommended two-question self-check (one line each, above the first CTA):**

- "If a customer asked today, do you know which [FRAMEWORK] requirements are met?"
- "If an RFP landed next week, would you wait 6 weeks for a baseline before answering?"

**Why this improves conversion:**
It reframes $299 against the *cost of delay* instead of the cost of compliance — a stronger comparison than "$299 vs. $5K audit." It forces the visitor to confront a specific, near-term trigger (customer review, RFP) rather than a vague future obligation. It does not change the existing offer or add unsupported claims. It can be tested as a small A/B section without touching the core $299 positioning.
