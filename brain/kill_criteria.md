# Kill Criteria

## When to Kill an Offer Lane

1. **No revenue after 30 days of active sales** → kill or pivot
2. **Negative margin after 60 days** → kill
3. **Churn > 40% monthly** → kill or radically redesign
4. **Delivery cost > 60% of revenue** → flag for automation improvement, kill if unfixable in 30 days
5. **Zero inbound interest after 2 weeks of outreach** → pivot positioning, kill if still dead at 4 weeks
6. **Client satisfaction < 3/5 average** → pause sales, fix delivery, reassess

## When to Scale an Offer Lane

1. **Positive margin + growing demand** → increase sales allocation
2. **Churn < 10%** → invest in upsell paths
3. **Delivery fully automated** → prioritize for productization
4. **3+ clients on same offer** → Productizer creates SKU

## Decision Authority
- Kill recommendation: any executive agent (Alex, Einstein, Forecaster)
- Kill approval: Simon
- Scale recommendation: Alex + Forecaster
- Scale execution: Einstein packages, Seller promotes
