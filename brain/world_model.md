# World Model

## Market Reality
- Businesses need AI but can't build it themselves.
- Most "AI agencies" sell labor, not autonomous systems.
- Recurring revenue beats project work.
- Trust + automation + grounding = defensible moat.

## Competitive Position
- OpenClaw gives us an autonomous agent backbone.
- 4-layer stack (LLM → RAG → Agent → Agentic AI) is the differentiator.
- Most competitors stop at Layer 1 or 2.

## Target Clients
- SMBs drowning in customer ops (support, follow-ups, booking, CRM)
- Companies needing AI governance (compliance, policy, audit)
- Verticals needing monitoring (regulatory, competitive, market)
- Anyone buying "AI tools" who actually needs "AI operations"

## Constraints
- Bootstrap phase — limited capital, must self-fund via early wins.
- Single operator (Simon) — automation is not optional, it's survival.
- Trust must be earned fast — delivery quality > marketing spend.
