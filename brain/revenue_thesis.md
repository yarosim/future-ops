# Revenue Thesis

## Core Principle
Sell only offers that work across all 4 layers. If an offer doesn't fit LLM + RAG + Agent + Agentic, it's not a core autonomous offer.

## Revenue Model
Recurring subscriptions > one-time projects. Every offer should convert into MRR.

## Offer Qualification Criteria

### LLM Fit
Can the offer use reasoning/generation well?

### RAG Fit
Can delivery and correspondence be grounded in reusable knowledge?

### Agent Fit
Can the workflow be executed by tools, APIs, and scripts?

### Agentic Fit
Can the system manage goals, memory, adaptation, and lifecycle with minimal human touch?

## Pricing Philosophy
- Value-based, not hourly.
- Anchor on outcome, not effort.
- Tiered: starter → growth → enterprise.
- Setup fees fund onboarding cost; MRR funds ongoing delivery.

## Revenue Targets
- Daily MRR contribution target: $2,740
- Monthly target: ~$83,000 MRR
- Path: 10-20 clients at $4,000-$8,000/mo average
