# Default Video Style Guide

## Purpose
Baseline visual and narrative style for all video productions unless client specifies otherwise.

---

## Visual Style
- **Default:** Cinematic realism with slight stylization
- **Color palette:** Warm tones, high contrast, professional color grading
- **Lighting:** Natural lighting preferred, cinematic key/fill/back for indoor scenes
- **Resolution target:** 4K minimum, 8K prompts for AI generation
- **Aspect ratios:**
  - YouTube long-form: 16:9
  - Shorts/Reels: 9:16
  - Social clips: 1:1 or 4:5

## Narrative Style
- **Tone:** Professional but engaging, not robotic
- **Pacing:** Moderate — not rushed, not draggy. Hook within first 5 seconds.
- **Hook structure:** Problem → tension → promise of resolution
- **Narration:** Clear, confident, conversational. Not corporate.
- **Music:** Subtle background score. Never overpowers narration.

## Branding Elements
- **Intro:** 3-5 second branded intro (logo + sound)
- **Outro:** CTA + subscribe + next video suggestion
- **Captions:** Always on. White text, dark shadow/background for readability.
- **Thumbnail:** High contrast, face (if applicable), 3-5 words max, bold font.

## Content Rules
- No misleading claims
- No fake urgency or scarcity
- No copyrighted music/visuals
- All content must be original or properly licensed
- Factual claims must be verifiable

## Platform-Specific Adjustments
### YouTube Long-Form
- 8-15 min sweet spot for most niches
- Chapters/timestamps in description
- End screen with CTA

### YouTube Shorts
- 30-60 seconds
- Vertical format
- Hook in first 1 second
- Single clear point per short

### LinkedIn/X Clips
- 60-90 seconds
- Subtitled (most watch muted)
- Value-first, CTA at end
