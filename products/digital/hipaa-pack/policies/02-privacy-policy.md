## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how [PRACTICE NAME] uses, discloses, and safeguards protected health information (PHI) in all forms — oral, paper, and electronic. It implements the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, Subparts A and E).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all workforce members (employees, contractors, students, and volunteers) of [PRACTICE NAME], and covers all PHI created, received, maintained, or transmitted by the Practice.

## 3. Definitions

- **PHI**: Individually identifiable health information transmitted or maintained in any form (45 CFR §160.103).
- **Use**: Sharing PHI within the entity.
- **Disclosure**: Sharing PHI outside the entity.
- **Minimum Necessary**: Limiting uses, disclosures, and requests for PHI to the least amount needed to accomplish the purpose (45 CFR §164.502(b)).

## 4. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

Workforce members may use and disclose PHI only for:

1. **Treatment** — providing care to the patient.
2. **Payment** — billing, claims, eligibility, collections.
3. **Health care operations** — quality assessment, training, audits, business planning.
4. **Uses authorized by the patient** in writing.
5. **Other purposes permitted or required by law** as described in the Notice of Privacy Practices (HPP-001).

Any use or disclosure not listed above requires review and written approval by the Privacy Officer.

## 5. Minimum Necessary Standard

- Except for treatment, and except for disclosures to or requests by the patient, staff must limit PHI use, disclosure, and requests to the minimum necessary.
- The Privacy Officer will maintain role-based access levels that define what PHI each role can access ([ROLES AND ACCESS LEVELS]).
- Requests for full medical records from third parties must specify the records requested; blanket requests for entire records should be queried when feasible.

## 6. Verification of Identity and Authority

Before disclosing PHI, staff must verify the identity of the requester and their authority to receive the information (45 CFR §164.514(h)), using at least one of:

- Photo identification
- Written request on organizational letterhead
- Callback to a known number of the requesting entity
- Confirmed patient designation/authorization on file

## 7. Patient Requests

- **Access requests (45 CFR §164.524)**: Respond within 30 calendar days (one 30-day extension permitted with written notice). Provide copies at a reasonable cost-based fee.
- **Amendment requests (45 CFR §164.526)**: Act within 60 days (30-day extension permitted).
- **Accounting of disclosures (45 CFR §164.528)**: Provide within 60 days.
- All requests must be in writing and logged in the [REQUEST LOG LOCATION/SYSTEM].

## 8. De-identification

PHI may be de-identified per 45 CFR §164.514(b) using either the Safe Harbor method (removal of 18 identifiers) or an expert determination. De-identified data may be used for quality improvement and analytics.

## 9. Documentation

All uses, disclosures, and requests outside treatment, payment, and operations must be logged with date, recipient, description, and purpose (45 CFR §164.528). Logs retained for at least [6] years.

- Violations are subject to the Sanctions Policy (HPP-007).
