## 1. Purpose

This Notice of Privacy Practices describes how [PRACTICE NAME] ("the Practice") may use and disclose your protected health information (PHI), your rights regarding your health information, and our legal obligations with respect to that information. This Notice is required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule, 45 CFR §164.520.

## 2. Our Legal Duties

The Practice is required by law to:

- Maintain the privacy and security of your PHI.
- Give you notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and make this Notice available to you.
- Notify you if a breach of your unsecured PHI occurs (45 CFR §164.404).
- Follow the duties and practices described in this Notice for as long as we hold your PHI.
- Make the Notice available on our website, if we maintain one, and provide an electronic copy on request.

## 3. How We May Use and Disclose Your Health Information

The following categories describe ways we may use and disclose your PHI without your authorization:

### 3.1 Treatment
We may use and disclose your PHI to provide, coordinate, or manage your healthcare. Example: [PHYSICIAN NAME] may share your medical history with a specialist consulting on your care.

### 3.2 Payment
We may use and disclose PHI to bill and collect payment from you, an insurance company, or a third party. Example: submitting claims to [PAYER NAME] for services rendered.

### 3.3 Health Care Operations
We may use PHI for quality assessment, staff training, licensing, audits, and business planning.

### 3.4 Other Permitted Uses (No Authorization Required)
- Business Associates performing services for the Practice under a Business Associate Agreement (45 CFR §164.504(e)).
- Public health activities, including reporting of communicable diseases to [STATE HEALTH DEPARTMENT].
- Reporting abuse, neglect, or domestic violence as required or permitted by law.
- Health oversight activities by government agencies.
- Judicial and administrative proceedings in response to a court order or subpoena.
- Law enforcement purposes, consistent with applicable law.
- Coroners, medical examiners, funeral directors, and organ donation organizations.
- Research, when approved by an Institutional Review Board or privacy board.
- To avert a serious threat to health or safety.
- Workers' compensation claims, as required by law.
- De-identified or limited data sets, as permitted by 45 CFR §164.514.

### 3.5 Uses Requiring Your Written Authorization
Uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes, uses for marketing, and any sale of PHI require your written authorization (45 CFR §164.508). Most uses and disclosures of PHI for other purposes, including sharing with third parties for reasons outside treatment, payment, and operations, also require authorization. You may revoke an authorization at any time in writing, except to the extent we have already acted in reliance on it.

### 3.6 Special Protections
Certain categories of information (HIV/AIDS status, substance use disorder treatment records under 42 CFR Part 2, mental health records, and genetic information) may have additional protections under [STATE] law. We will comply with the stricter requirement.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your PHI (45 CFR §164.520(b)(1)):

1. **Inspect and copy** your medical record. Submit a written request to [CONTACT ADDRESS]. We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee of no more than [FEE AMOUNT/PER-PAGE RATE] per page.
2. **Amend** your record. If we deny your request, we will explain why in writing, and you may submit a statement of disagreement.
3. **Accounting of disclosures** — a list of certain disclosures we made, available once per 12-month period at no charge.
4. **Request restrictions** on uses/disclosures. We are **required** to agree to a request to restrict disclosure of PHI to a health plan for payment or operations if the disclosure is for a service for which you paid in full out-of-pocket (45 CFR §164.522(a)(1)(vi)).
5. **Request confidential communications** at an alternate address or phone number.
6. **Paper copy of this Notice** — available at any time upon request.
7. **Breach notification** — we will notify you of any breach of your unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days from discovery.
8. **Complain** if you feel your privacy rights have been violated (see Section 6).
