## 1. Purpose

To specify the proper functions to be performed, the manner in which they are performed, and the physical attributes of workstations that access ePHI at [PRACTICE NAME], per 45 CFR §164.310(b) and §164.310(c).

## 2. Approved Workstations & Functions

| Workstation Location | Permitted Functions | ePHI Access |
|---|---|---|
| [Exam rooms / provider workstations] | EHR charting | Full clinical |
| [Front desk] | Scheduling, check-in, payment | Demographic/scheduling |
| [Billing office] | Claims, payments | Billing-related PHI |
| [Admin office] | Management, HR | Admin PHI |
| [Personal/remote devices] | Only via VPN + MFA | Role-limited |

## 3. Workstation Use Rules

1. Lock your screen whenever you step away ([Win+L] / auto-lock after [10–15] minutes).
2. Do not share workstation credentials.
3. Do not install personal software on practice workstations.
4. Do not disable or bypass security software (antivirus, firewall, encryption).
5. Log off completely at end of shift.
6. PHI may not be accessed on public computers (hotel, library, airport kiosks).

## 4. Screen Visibility

- Position monitors away from waiting areas and windows.
- Use privacy filters where required.
- No patient information visible on paper notes taped to monitors or desks in public-facing areas.

## 5. Physical Security

- Workstations are secured to [DESKS/CABINETS] where feasible; laptops use cable locks or are stored in locked cabinets overnight.
- Servers and network gear are in [LOCKED LOCATION].

## 6. Clean Desk

- Paper PHI is stored in locked drawers at end of day.
- No PHI left on desks, printers, or fax machines unattended.

## 7. Software & Patching

- Operating systems and applications are patched on [MONTHLY] schedule by [IT VENDOR/SECURITY OFFICER].
- Antivirus/EDR runs on all endpoints and is kept current.
- Unsupported (end-of-life) operating systems are prohibited on any device touching ePHI.
