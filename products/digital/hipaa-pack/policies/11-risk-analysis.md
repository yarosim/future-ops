## 1. Purpose

The HIPAA Security Rule requires [PRACTICE NAME] to "conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of the potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information" (§164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)). This policy establishes how and when that analysis is performed.

The risk analysis is the foundation of the Security Rule — OCR's enforcement actions most often cite a missing or inadequate risk analysis.

## 2. Frequency

- A full risk analysis is performed [ANNUALLY].
- An updated analysis is triggered by: new EHR or major software change, office move, new IT vendor, workforce change affecting systems, or a significant incident.

## 3. Scope of Assets to Inventory

All systems and devices that create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI:

- EHR / practice management system ([VENDOR/SYSTEM])
- Servers, workstations, laptops, tablets, phones
- Network equipment (router, Wi-Fi, firewall)
- Email, fax, voicemail
- Cloud services (file storage, telehealth, backup, billing/clearinghouse)
- Paper records and their storage locations (transition points to electronic)
- External media (USB drives, CDs)

## 4. Methodology (7 Steps)

1. **Inventory** all ePHI assets and their locations (physical and cloud).
2. **Document threats** to each asset (theft, loss, unauthorized access, malware, power failure, natural disaster, vendor outage).
3. **Document vulnerabilities** (missing encryption, outdated software, weak passwords, untrained staff, no backup).
4. **Assess likelihood and impact** of each threat/vulnerability pair (High / Medium / Low).
5. **Determine risk level** for each pair.
6. **Identify safeguards** already in place and gaps.
7. **Document a remediation plan** with owners and target dates; track to completion.

The full worksheet is at: [RISK ASSESSMENT WORKSHEET FILE — see risk-assessment-worksheet.md in this pack].

## 5. Documentation & Retention

The completed risk analysis, asset inventory, and remediation plan are retained for at least [6] years and stored in [LOCATION]. A summary of findings is presented to [OWNER/MANAGER] within [2 WEEKS] of completion.

## 6. Remediation Tracking

Open remediation items are reviewed [QUARTERLY]. Items overdue more than [60] days are escalated to the Practice Owner. Completed items are verified by the Security Officer and documented with dates.
