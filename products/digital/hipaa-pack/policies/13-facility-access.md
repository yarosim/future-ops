## 1. Purpose

To limit physical access to [PRACTICE NAME] facilities and systems containing ePHI while permitting properly authorized access, per 45 CFR §164.310(a).

## 2. Physical Access Controls

### 2.1 Entry Points
- Exterior doors are [LOCKED DURING BUSINESS HOURS except main entrance / locked at all times with badge access].
- After-hours access is limited to [AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL] via [KEY/BADGE/CODE].
- Keys and badges are inventoried; lost items are reported to [MANAGER] within 24 hours and replaced/re-keyed as needed.

### 2.2 Visitors
- All visitors check in at [RECEPTION] and are escorted or observed in non-public areas.
- Vendors and IT personnel sign in and out in the [VISITOR LOG]; log retained [1 YEAR].
- Delivery personnel remain in reception unless escorted.

### 2.3 Server/Network Equipment
- If on-site equipment exists, it is housed in [LOCKED ROOM/CABINET] accessible only to [AUTHORIZED ROLES].
- Room/lock inventory documented; access list reviewed [SEMI-ANNUALLY].

## 3. Workstation Placement (§164.310(b))

- Workstations displaying ePHI are positioned so screens are not visible from waiting areas or public hallways.
- Privacy filters applied where line-of-sight cannot be avoided.
- Reception monitors use privacy filters and screensavers with automatic lock.

## 4. Device & Media Controls (§164.310(c))

- Secured areas for storage of paper records and media containing ePHI: [FILE ROOM LOCATION(S)].
- Access to these areas is limited to [AUTHORIZED ROLES] and logged where feasible.

## 5. Maintenance & Repairs

- Facilities maintenance or IT repairs affecting systems containing ePHI require escort by [AUTHORIZED STAFF].
- Repairs are documented in [MAINTENANCE LOG].

## 6. Key Events

- Termination: keys/badges retrieved per the Access Control termination checklist (HPP-005).
- Breach of facility security (break-in, unauthorized entry): report to Security Officer; assess whether ePHI was affected; follow Incident Response (HPP-009).
