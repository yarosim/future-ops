## BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

- **[PRACTICE NAME]**, a [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE], with offices at [ADDRESS] ("Covered Entity"); and
- **[VENDOR NAME]**, with offices at [ADDRESS] ("Business Associate").

### 1. Definitions
Terms not defined here have the meanings in HIPAA (45 CFR §160.103). "Business Associate" generally means a person or entity that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of a Covered Entity.

### 2. Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only:
(a) as necessary to perform the services specified in [SERVICE AGREEMENT/CONTRACT NAME]; or
(b) as required by law.

Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in any manner not permitted by this Agreement, and shall not de-identify, aggregate, or use PHI for its own purposes (including product development or marketing) except as permitted by HIPAA.

### 3. Obligations of Business Associate
Business Associate agrees to:
1. Use appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with 45 CFR §164.314 and its own Security Rule obligations (§164.302–§164.318).
2. Report to Covered Entity any security incident or breach of unsecured PHI within [BUSINESS ASSOCIATE HAS 60 DAYS / 5 DAYS — negotiate] of discovery, including what happened, whose PHI was involved, and mitigation steps.
3. Ensure its subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI agree in writing to the same restrictions (§164.502(e)(1)(ii)).
4. Make PHI available to Covered Entity for individual access requests (§164.524), amendment (§164.526), and accounting of disclosures (§164.528).
5. Make its internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of HHS for compliance review.
6. Return or destroy all PHI upon termination of the Agreement where feasible (§164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J)), and certify destruction.

### 4. Obligations of Covered Entity
Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices that affects Business Associate's use or disclosure of PHI, and of any changes in, or revocation of, permission by an individual.

### 5. Termination
This Agreement terminates on the date the underlying service agreement terminates. Covered Entity may terminate this Agreement immediately for a material breach that Business Associate fails to cure within [30] days of written notice.

### 6. Term
Effective [DATE] and remains in effect until all PHI is returned or destroyed or the Agreement is terminated.
