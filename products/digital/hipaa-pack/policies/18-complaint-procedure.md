## 1. Purpose

To establish a process for receiving and responding to complaints about the Practice's privacy and security practices, and to inform individuals about the complaint process, as required by 45 CFR §164.530(d).

## 2. How to File a Complaint

Complaints may be submitted:

- **In person** to: [PRIVACY OFFICER NAME], [OFFICE LOCATION]
- **By phone**: [PHONE]
- **By email**: [EMAIL]
- **By mail**: [PRACTICE ADDRESS]
- **Anonymously**: [METHOD, e.g., sealed envelope addressed to Privacy Officer]

A written complaint form is available at [LOCATION / WEBSITE]. Individuals are not required to use the form; any written or verbal complaint is accepted.

## 3. Process

1. **Receipt**: The Privacy Officer logs the complaint in the Complaint Log ([LOCATION]) within [2 BUSINESS DAYS], with date, complainant, description, and category.
2. **Investigation**: The Privacy Officer investigates promptly — target resolution within [30 DAYS]. Investigation may include interviews, record review, and audit log review.
3. **Response**: The complainant receives a written response describing the outcome and any corrective action taken, without revealing PHI of other patients.
4. **Corrective action**: If a violation is found, remediation is applied (policy change, retraining, sanctions per HPP-007).
5. **Escalation**: Complaints involving potential breach trigger the Incident Response (HPP-009) and Breach Notification (HPP-010) processes.

## 4. Right to Complain Externally

Individuals may also complain directly to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights:

- OCR Complaint Portal: https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/portal/lobby.jsf
- OCR Region: [OCR REGION — see regional address in Notice of Privacy Practices]

The Practice will never retaliate against any person for filing a complaint (§164.530(g)).

## 5. Recordkeeping

Complaint records are retained for at least [6] years and summarized [ANNUALLY] for trend review (repeated issues in one area signal a training or process gap).
