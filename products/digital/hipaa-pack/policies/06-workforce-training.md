## 1. Purpose

To ensure every workforce member of [PRACTICE NAME] understands HIPAA privacy and security requirements and the Practice's policies before accessing PHI.

## 2. Who Must Be Trained

All workforce members, including employees, contractors, temporary staff, students, and volunteers. Business associates are responsible for training their own staff (confirmed via BAA).

## 3. Training Requirements

### 3.1 New Hire
- Privacy and security awareness training completed **within [X days of hire] and before independent access to PHI**.
- Topics: PHI basics, minimum necessary, patient rights, incident reporting, email/device rules, sanctions.

### 3.2 Annual Refresher
- All staff complete refresher training [ANNUALLY] during [MONTH].

### 3.3 Material Change Training
- When policies or systems materially change, affected staff are trained "promptly" per §164.530(b)(2)(ii)(B) — target within [30] days.

### 3.4 Role-Specific Training
- Providers: patient rights, authorizations, psychotherapy notes, records requests.
- Billing: payment disclosures, minimum necessary.
- Front desk: verification of identity, incidental disclosures, directory opt-outs.

## 4. Curriculum (Minimum Topics)

1. What PHI is and where it lives in the practice.
2. Minimum necessary standard.
3. Patient rights under the Notice of Privacy Practices.
4. Incident and breach reporting — how, to whom, how fast.
5. Workstation, mobile device, and email rules.
6. Social media prohibition on patient information.
7. Sanctions for noncompliance.

## 5. Delivery & Documentation

- Training is delivered via [METHOD: in-person / LMS / videos + quiz].
- A passing score of [80%+] is required on post-training assessments.
- Completion records (date, attendee, topic, score) are retained for at least [6] years in [LOCATION].

## 6. Tracking

The Privacy Officer maintains a training matrix and reports completion status to [OWNER/MANAGER] [QUARTERLY]. Staff with overdue training receive a written reminder; continued noncompletion triggers the Sanctions Policy (HPP-007).
