## 1. Purpose

To establish sanctions for workforce members who fail to comply with the privacy and security policies of [PRACTICE NAME], as required by 45 CFR §164.530(e).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all workforce members. Sanctions apply regardless of whether the violation caused a reportable breach.

## 3. Violation Categories

### Tier 1 — Minor / Unintentional
Examples: leaving a workstation unlocked briefly; discussing patient care within hearing distance of others; minor documentation lapses.
Typical sanction: [VERBAL counseling + retraining, documented in file].

### Tier 2 — Serious
Examples: repeated Tier 1 violations; snooping on records of family, coworkers, or public figures without a work reason; emailing PHI to wrong recipient due to carelessness; failing to report a known incident.
Typical sanction: [WRITTEN warning + retraining + access review; possible suspension].

### Tier 3 — Severe / Intentional
Examples: intentional unauthorized disclosure; selling or misusing PHI; falsifying records; accessing records to harm or embarrass; deleting logs to conceal activity.
Typical sanction: [SUSPENSION or TERMINATION, and referral to legal authorities where required].

## 4. Process

1. Any workforce member may report a suspected violation to the Privacy Officer (see Complaint Procedure, HPP-018). Reports may be anonymous.
2. The Privacy Officer investigates promptly and documents findings.
3. The Privacy Officer and [PRACTICE OWNER/MANAGER] determine the sanction tier.
4. The sanction is documented in the personnel file with date, description, and corrective action.
5. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a Tier 3 violation (§164.530(g)).

## 5. Documentation & Retention

Sanction records are retained for [6] years and reviewed [ANNUALLY] for patterns that indicate training or process gaps.

## 6. Intentional Non-Retaliation Statement

The Practice may not intimidate, threaten, coerce, harass, or retaliate against any individual for filing a complaint, participating in an investigation, or opposing any unlawful practice.
