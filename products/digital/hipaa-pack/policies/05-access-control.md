## 1. Purpose

To define how [PRACTICE NAME] establishes, grants, modifies, and terminates access to systems and PHI, ensuring only authorized individuals access ePHI.

## 2. Access Principles

1. **Unique user identification** (§164.312(a)(2)(i)): Every user has a unique account. Shared logins are prohibited.
2. **Role-based access**: Access is granted based on job role, not individual preference.
3. **Least privilege**: Users get the minimum access necessary to perform their duties.
4. **Emergency access**: An "emergency mode" procedure exists (§164.312(a)(2)(ii)) — [DESCRIBE BREAK-GLASS PROCEDURE].

## 3. Account Provisioning

- New accounts are created only upon written authorization from [HIRING MANAGER/OFFICER] and completion of onboarding paperwork.
- Default credentials are changed before first use.
- Users must sign the Confidentiality Acknowledgment before receiving credentials.

## 4. Access Modification

Access is re-reviewed whenever a user changes roles. The Security Officer conducts an access review [SEMI-ANNUALLY] comparing active accounts against current staffing (§164.308(a)(3)(ii)(B)).

## 5. Account Termination

- Upon termination, resignation, or role change, access is disabled within [24 HOURS / SAME BUSINESS DAY] (§164.308(a)(3)(ii)(C)).
- The terminating manager must notify IT/[SECURITY OFFICER] on or before the termination date.
- Termination checklist: disable accounts, retrieve devices/keys/badges, retrieve remote-access tokens, confirm no forwarding of practice email to personal accounts.

## 6. Authentication Standards

- Passwords: minimum [12] characters; forced change on first login; [MFA enabled].
- Automatic logoff after [10–15] minutes of inactivity on EHR workstations (§164.312(a)(2)(iii)).
- No password sharing; no writing passwords on paper near workstations.

## 7. Remote Access

- Remote access to systems containing ePHI requires VPN + MFA.
- Remote sessions are logged.

## 8. Emergency Access

If the EHR is unavailable or a provider needs urgent access, follow the [BREAK-GLASS PROCEDURE DOCUMENT LOCATION]. All emergency access is logged and reviewed by the Security Officer within [3] business days.
