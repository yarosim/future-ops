## How to Customize (30–60 minutes)

1. **Search for `[` brackets** — every placeholder is bracketed. Fill in practice name, officer names, dates, vendors, and contact info first.
2. **Adjust the choices** — some fields offer options like `[MONTHLY/QUARTERLY]`. Pick what your practice will actually do. Don't commit to a review cycle you won't follow; auditors check whether you did what your policy says.
3. **Complete the risk assessment worksheet** — do this before finalizing policies 03, 09, 11, 12, and 16, since your findings determine your safeguards.
4. **Collect signatures** — each policy has a sign-off block. Unsigned policies carry little weight.
5. **Train your team** — policy 06 tells you how; store completion records.
6. **Set a review date** — annually, and after any system change.
