# Autonomous AI Governance Ops

## Description
A subscription service providing automated AI governance, compliance monitoring, and risk management.

## Layers 
### LLM
- Generates policy drafts, compliance reports, evidence summaries.

### RAG
- Approved governance rules, policy templates, risk libraries, client configurations, past audits.

### AI Agent (Tom, Monitor, Support Triage)
- Collects evidence, runs approval workflows, sends reminders, packages reports, flags compliance gaps.

### Agentic AI (Alex, Tom, Memory Librarian)
- Monitors changes, updates rule sets, schedules reviews, identifies upsells and gaps, maintains governance knowledge base.

## Target Revenue
- High-trust subscription lane, potential for higher MRR per client due to complexity and criticality.
