# Secure Agent Governance — Commercial Offer

## Positioning

**Your AI agents are privileged accounts. Treat them that way.**

Know what agents exist, restrict what they can do, and produce defensible evidence before they touch sensitive data or production systems. YaRo Security works with the current stack; platform replacement is not required.

## Commercial ladder

### 1) Agent Privilege Audit — $750 fixed fee

A focused remote assessment for one priority agent/workflow.

**Includes**
- Intake and one 60-minute discovery session
- Review of ownership, identity, systems/data, connectors, read/write permissions, approval gates, logging, stop/recovery, and available evidence
- Red/yellow/green score with critical overrides
- Up to 10 prioritized findings
- Executive risk memo and 30-minute readout

**Timing:** 3 business days after complete intake and evidence access.  
**Credit:** $750 credited toward a qualifying Governance Sprint contracted within 30 calendar days.  
**Best for:** Establishing urgency and scope before implementation.

A 10-business-day control-design and evidence sprint.

**Starter — $4,500**
- Up to 3 agents/workflows
- Up to 5 connected systems/tools
- One business unit and one primary data boundary
- Registry, permission matrix, risk tiering, approval design, logging/recovery review
- Top-10 findings, governance standard, and 30/60/90-day roadmap

**Expanded — $8,500**
- Up to 8 agents/workflows
- Up to 12 connected systems/tools
- Up to 3 business units or environments
- Adds deeper tool/data-flow mapping, exception register, evidence index, and two control-design workshops

**Included deliverables**
- Validated agent/MCP/plugin inventory and named owners
- Agent registry and permission matrix
- Tool-call and data-flow map based on supplied architecture/evidence
- Risk tiers and human-approval conditions
- Logging, retention, kill-switch, rollback, and incident-path review
- Control mapping to agreed references (for example, NIST/OWASP/CMMC-relevant practices); mapping is not certification
- Executive report and prioritized remediation roadmap

Price and schedule depend on platforms, environments, vendor constraints, and change-control requirements. Production changes require written authorization and an approved change window.

Recurring governance for an agreed agent inventory:

## Delivery timeline

**Before Day 1:** Signed agreement, payment terms satisfied, intake complete, stakeholders scheduled, and read-only evidence access available.  
**Days 1–2:** Kickoff, inventory, owner and purpose validation.  
**Days 3–5:** Identity/permission, connector, and data-flow review.  
**Days 6–7:** Approval, logging, stop/recovery, and evidence review.  
**Days 8–9:** Findings validation and roadmap.  
**Day 10:** Executive readout and handoff.

Client delays, unavailable evidence, or expanded scope may shift delivery dates.

## Client responsibilities

- Appoint an executive sponsor and technical owner.
- Provide accurate inventory, diagrams, policies, logs, screenshots/configuration exports, and vendor documentation.
- Arrange access using least privilege; do not send secrets through ordinary email or forms.
- Confirm lawful authority to share information and assess the systems.
- Review findings and accept/decline proposed changes; retain operational decision authority.

## Exclusions

Unless separately agreed in writing, this engagement does **not** include:
- Legal advice, regulatory interpretation, attestation, certification, C3PAO assessment, SOC audit, or guarantee of compliance/security
- Penetration testing, red teaming, source-code review, model evaluation, or continuous runtime monitoring
- Production configuration changes or remediation under the Audit/Sprint fee
- Full enterprise discovery, forensic investigation, incident response, vendor assurance, or data classification
- Review of agents, systems, tenants, connectors, business units, or frameworks above stated limits
- Travel, third-party licensing, cloud/vendor charges, or custom software development

## Assumptions and change control

Findings are based on evidence and access available during the engagement and represent a point-in-time assessment. Unknown or unavailable evidence is recorded as a risk, not assumed compliant. Scope changes require a written change order. YaRo Security will not request or retain raw secrets when safer evidence is available.
