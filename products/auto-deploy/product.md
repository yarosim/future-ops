# Auto-Deployed Products

## Description
A scalable layer for digital products that actively deploy and manage themselves, providing ongoing value.

## Layers
### LLM
- Generates tailored output, setup guidance, and launch notes.

### RAG
- Supplies templates, implementation logic, product documentation, and onboarding logic.

### AI Agent (Fulfillment, Correspondence, Billing)
- Collects intake, provisions assets, sends credentials, launches workflows, handles payments.

### Agentic AI (Alex, Einstein, Productizer)
- Tracks activation, triggers nudges, monitors usage, pushes upgrade prompts, manages product lifecycle.

## Target Revenue
- Digital products that activate into recurring value beyond initial download.
