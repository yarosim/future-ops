# Campaign Engine — Revenue Lane G

## Overview
A repeatable marketing campaign orchestra that converts founder thinking, swipe data, and market signals into revenue through structured traffic → nurture → launch → convert → recycle loops.

NOT a content bot. This is a campaign system designer that builds and operates the full revenue machine.

## Core Insight
AI is not just for asset generation — it is for campaign system design. The valuable move is not "make some posts" but "build a repeatable revenue machine" with roles for traffic, nurture, launch, conversion, and feedback.

The real moat is packaging founder thinking, marketing knowledge, swipe data, and campaign sequencing into a reusable operating system. AI speeds up execution, but revenue comes from the structure.

## The Campaign Framework

### 1. Traffic → Get Attention
Organic or paid. Hooks, shorts, podcasts, X/LinkedIn, YouTube, SEO.

### 2. Holding Pattern → Warm People Up
Newsletter, drip content, lead magnets, retargeting. Don't push the offer immediately.

### 3. Selling Event → Run the Push
Webinars, VSLs, 3-4 email bursts, landing pages, call booking pushes, demos.

### 4. Conversion → Get the Sale
Checkout, trial onboarding, follow-up, objection handling, abandoned-cart recovery.

### 5. Recycle Non-Buyers → Back to Nurture
Non-buyers go back into the holding pattern until the next selling event.

## The 6-Agent Campaign Orchestra

### 1. Traffic Agent (traffic-agent)
Mines trends, creates hooks, books podcasts, pushes content to X/LinkedIn/YouTube.
- Input: market signals, niche research, audience data
- Output: traffic_plan.md, hooks/, content_calendar.md

### 2. Holding Pattern Agent (holding-pattern)
Runs newsletter, drip content, lead magnets, and retargeting audiences.
- Input: new leads from traffic, content assets
- Output: nurture_sequences/, lead_magnets/, retargeting_rules.md

### 3. Selling Event Agent (selling-event)
Launches webinars, VSLs, email bursts, landing pages, and call booking pushes.
- Input: warmed audience from holding pattern, offer details
- Output: launch_plan.md, email_sequences/, landing_pages/, webinar_scripts/

### 4. Conversion Agent (conversion-agent)
Handles checkout, trial onboarding, follow-up, objections, abandoned-cart recovery.
- Input: prospects from selling event
- Output: conversion_flows/, objection_scripts.md, recovery_sequences/

### 5. Swipe/Memory Agent (swipe-memory)
Stores competitor emails, winning hooks, CTAs, angles, and offer tests.
- Input: all campaign outputs + competitor research
- Output: swipe_library/, winning_patterns.md, angle_archive/

### 6. Campaign Analyst Agent (campaign-analyst)
Reads performance, identifies drop-off points, and rewrites weak steps.
- Input: campaign metrics across all stages
- Output: performance_report.md, drop_off_analysis.md, rewrite_recommendations.md
