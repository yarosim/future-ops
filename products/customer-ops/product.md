# Autonomous Customer Ops

## Description
A recurring subscription service focused on automating customer interactions, lead management, and support.

## Layers
### LLM
- Drafts sales copy, support replies, lead classifications.

### RAG
- Product catalog, FAQs, offer definitions, client histories, brand voice.

### AI Agent (Seller, Correspondence, Support Triage, Billing)
- Manages outreach, follow-ups, CRM updates, task routing, payment collection, renewals. 

### Agentic AI (Alex, Einstein, Forecaster)
- Optimizes response timing, tests offers, updates scripts, monitors conversion, manages lifecycle.

## Target Revenue
- Aim for $4,000-$8,000 MRR per client.

## Status
- **Phase:** To be developed. Priority 1 for revenue generation.
